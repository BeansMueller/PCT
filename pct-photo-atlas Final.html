Locations are estimates. None of the album's photos carry GPS, so each mile comes from the photo's local time, that day's start and camp miles from your log, and a 2.5 mph pace from a 6 AM start, capped at camp. Miles are FarOut 2022 miles from your log. Coordinates come from Halfmile's 2018 track, lined up with FarOut at 23 landmarks (highway crossings, towns, Harts Pass and the monument) so reroutes since 2018 don't shift photos. Photos of the hundred-mile markers you built are pinned to their exact mile, and the rest of those days are re-timed from the marker. Elsewhere, expect a few miles of drift on long days, town stops, side trips (Whitney on 5/30, Kearsarge on 5/31 and 6/2, Green Lakes on 7/24) and the 7/30 fire-closure skip.
